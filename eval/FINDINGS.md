## Findings (written after reading the tables; no prompt, threshold or weight was changed after seeing eval results)

Test set: 43 eval photos (13 nameplates + 13 dark copies, 9 damage + 8 dark copies) and 12 notes. Photos are openly licensed web images (`data/web/labels.csv`) plus synthetic dark copies, not frames from our glasses - the mock camera only gave us a handful of real frames, so "our own frames" is the honest gap in this eval. 55 Gemini calls (gemini-3.1-flash-lite), cached.

1. **Nameplates: two readers beat one.** ML Kit alone gets 31-92% per field; with Gemini as second reader 77-100% (make 85%, model 77%, serial 77%, date 100%, n=13 each). Wrong answers carry clearly lower confidence than right ones on every nameplate field (e.g. serial 0.28 vs 0.69): when the two readers disagree or only one reads the field, confidence is cut.
2. **Damage type is the weak field: 56% (5/9), and its confidence does not go down when wrong (0.90 vs 0.95).** It has a single reader, so the model's self-confidence is all we have, and that is known to be untrustworthy (Phase 5 smoke test). Three of four failures are frames with more than one kind of damage where the single-label ground truth picked another one. Until a second signal exists, damage line items must not be treated as final: the back office should review them. Next step: multi-label output with top-2 probabilities, margin as confidence.
3. **Low light hurts, and confidence says so.** Nameplate fields drop from 85% (44/52) to 71% (37/52) on dark copies; damage from 56% (5/9) to 38% (3/8). Confidence on dark frames roughly halves (nameplate correct 0.68 -> 0.35), so the drop is visible to the back office. This is the evidence for a light on the glasses / asking the tech to turn on a light.
4. **The quality gate helps.** Frames the gate rejects are less accurate (nameplate 73% vs 83%, damage 38% vs 56%) and carry about half the confidence. Asking for a retake on those frames is the right call.
5. **Calibration rises with confidence overall** (0-0.5: 59% of 51, 0.5-0.8: 86% of 29, 0.8-1.0: 89% of 37), driven by nameplates and notes. Damage type is the exception (see 2).
6. **Notes:** typed notes 96-100%; real ASR in noise recovered 7/8 fields ("drywall behind soft wallet" -> behind the toilet / soft_spot / drywall). The quiet-room garble "what are heated looking at base" (water heater leaking) could not be recovered: 1/4, but every wrong field had confidence <= 0.48.
7. **Size is not measured here:** no eval-split damage photo has a tape-measured size (only one tune photo does). Phase 8 part A measures it on our own reference-object photos.
8. **Ground-truth issues found, left as scored:** web_np_19 make - Gemini transcribed the Greek plate exactly as printed ("ΣΤΑΓΙΕΡ ΝΤΑΙΜΛΕΡ" = Steyr-Daimler), counted wrong against the Latin label. Damage labels are single-class although several frames show two damages (see T6).
